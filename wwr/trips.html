Our Rafting Trips

Choose the perfect adventure for your skill level and experience the thrill of white water rafting.

Trip List

Beginner Rapids

Perfect for first-time rafters and families. Enjoy gentle class rapids while learning basic paddling skills with our expert guides. A fun and safe introduction to white water rafting.

Duration: 2 hours  |  Difficulty: Easy

Canyon Adventure

An exciting intermediate trip through scenic canyons with class rapids. Great for those who have some experience and want more challenge while still enjoying the beauty of the river.

Duration: 4 hours  |  Difficulty: Moderate

Extreme White Water

Our most challenging trip featuring powerful class rapids. Designed for experienced rafters looking for an adrenaline-filled adventure with professional guides.

Duration: Full day  |  Difficulty: Advanced
